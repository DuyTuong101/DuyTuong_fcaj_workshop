First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) has given me one of the most memorable and meaningful experiences of my life. Looking back on my 10-week internship, I am truly grateful to be a part of this program. Below are my detailed feelings and feedback based on my journey.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
I was immediately welcomed into a community of like-minded peers. Everyone shares a deep passion for technology, especially cloud computing and AI/ML. This shared mindset creates a highly comfortable and open atmosphere, making it incredibly easy to share ideas, ask tough questions, and just chat casually. Whenever we faced challenges, I never felt alone because I knew there were always people nearby with the exact same goals and enthusiasm.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentors and senior members here are truly outstanding. They are always willing to guide us with immense patience and detail. If I encountered a bug or a complex architectural concept, they would not just hand me the solution, but instead walk me through the problem step-by-step, helping me build the right logic and mindset. This supportive, "senior-to-junior" approach made a massive difference in my learning curve.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The 4-week AQI capstone project fit perfectly with my ML academic background. It was the ideal bridge between university theory and real-world application, introducing me to the practical complexities of deploying models in production using AWS, which is exactly what I needed to boost my confidence as a future Engineer.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
One aspect I highly appreciate is the high-quality training materials. The mentors have prepared a series of video tutorials that are incredibly well-structured, easy to follow, and visually engaging. Instead of feeling dry or boring, the videos hooked my attention. Combined with the hands-on workshops, I absorbed a huge amount of practical skills, from containerization to advanced MLOps, in a very short time.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The team spirit is exceptional. There is an undeniable sense of support and camaraderie here. Regardless of our roles, everyone treats each other as teammates, ready to help out when someone gets stuck. The culture encourages collaboration rather than competition, making me feel like a valued member of the "big family" rather than just a temporary intern.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program provided flexible schedules, an internship allowance, and, most importantly, access to top-tier technical training sessions. The benefits far exceeded my expectations for a student internship.

### Additional Questions
- **What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
  The people. The combination of a welcoming, like-minded community and the enthusiastic support from senior mentors made me look forward to every single day at work.
  
- **What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**  
  I personally find it already excellent. Maybe there could be a few more offline team-building trips (like the AWS Knowledge Battle we had) to let the different teams physically bond even more deeply.
  
- **If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**  
  Absolutely yes. It's not just a place to write code; it's a place to grow as an engineer and as a person, surrounded by the best mentors and peers.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations
- **Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**  
  I only hope that the program continues to be maintained and widely promoted. There are so many students out there with a passion for cloud technology who could benefit immensely from this incredible environment.
  
- **Would you like to continue this program in the future?**  
  Yes, I would be honored to come back, perhaps as a mentor one day to "pass the torch" to the next generation of FCAJ interns, just like I was guided.
  
- **Any other comments (free sharing):**  
  Thank you, FCAJ. Please continue to grow and expand. I firmly believe that this is a truly unique community, and I hope to see it influence and nurture even more young Vietnamese tech enthusiasts in the future.
