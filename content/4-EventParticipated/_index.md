Throughout my internship at the First Cloud AI Journey program, I actively participated in three enriching events organized by the AWS Vietnam community and the internship team. These events were not only opportunities to expand my technical network but also provided invaluable insights into real-world IT operations, team collaboration, and cloud expertise. Below is a detailed breakdown of each event.

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)  
**Event Name:** DevOps Career Path Sharing: From IT Helpdesk to Enterprise DevOps  

**Date & Time:** 09:00 - 11:00, June 6, 2026  

**Location:** Online Meeting (AWS Chime / Zoom)  

**Role:** Attendee  

**Event Description:**  
This session featured a seasoned IT professional who shared their inspiring journey transitioning from a traditional IT Helpdesk support role to becoming a Senior DevOps Engineer for large-scale enterprises. The speaker covered essential topics such as building the right mindset for operations, mastering scripting (Bash/Python), and diving deep into the core DevOps ecosystem (CI/CD pipelines, Docker, Kubernetes, and infrastructure as code). 

**Outcomes & Value Gained:**  
Gained a clear understanding of the DevOps career roadmap and the critical soft skills required for the role. This event motivated me to look beyond just model training in my ML role, encouraging me to think about the full lifecycle and operationalization of AI/ML systems on AWS.

---

### [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)  
**Event Name:** Tech Tools & Containerization Workshop: Matching Tools to Roles  

**Date & Time:** 09:00 - 11:30, June 13, 2026  

**Location:** Online Meeting (AWS Chime / Zoom)  

**Role:** Attendee  

**Event Description:**  
An interactive workshop focusing on the software development lifecycle within a modern tech team. The first half of the event analyzed the most suitable toolchains for different positions (Developers, Ops Engineers, Data/ML Engineers). The second half was a hands-on technical deep dive into Containerization concepts, explaining the fundamental architecture of Docker, container networking, and the basics of orchestration for deploying scalable applications.

**Outcomes & Value Gained:**  
Provided me with a practical understanding of how the tools I use for ML (like SageMaker containers) fit into the broader DevOps ecosystem. The container knowledge was particularly beneficial for understanding how to package custom model training environments and ensure reproducibility across different AWS instances.

---

### [Event 3](4.3-Event3/)  
**Event Name:** AWS Knowledge Battle: Inter-group Competition  

**Date & Time:** 14:00 - 16:30, June 20, 2026  

**Location:** Online Meeting (AWS Chime / Zoom)  

**Role:** Attendee  

**Event Description:**  
An exciting and fast-paced knowledge competition held between various intern teams. Teams competed against each other in a quiz format covering a wide range of AWS topics, including core services (EC2, S3, VPC), security best practices, serverless architectures, and cost optimization strategies. The competition required rapid collaboration and quick thinking under pressure.

**Outcomes & Value Gained:**  
This was a fantastic team-building experience that strengthened our group's communication skills. It also served as a thorough revision of AWS core concepts, highlighting areas where my team needed to improve. The competitive atmosphere pushed us to think critically and quickly recall architectural trade-offs in real-world scenarios.
